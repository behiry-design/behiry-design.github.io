Who I Am

I'm a fourth-year Mechatronics & Automation Engineering student at Ain Shams University, Cairo (expected graduation 2027), with a GPA of 3.19/4.0. Growing up in Port Said gave me an early awareness of the gap between engineering potential and real community needs — and a conviction that applying the right control to any system can produce dramatic improvements that reflect on people's lives.

This belief drives everything I build. My work spans autonomous robotics (ROS2, Gazebo, SLAM, YOLOv8), control systems (PID, cascaded control, Simulink, Simscape), embedded real-time systems (FreeRTOS, ARM Cortex-M, ESP32), industrial automation (TIA Portal, S7-1500 PLC), and biomedical engineering (EMG signal processing, prosthetic hand control).

I've pursued learning beyond my home institution — selected for a DAAD-funded program at TU Berlin (grade 1.0), the UCLan Intelligent Machines Engineering Programme in the UK, an ARM internship at Garrio LLC, and serving as a UNDP Youth for Sustainability Ambassador. Each experience reinforced that an engineer's value is measured by what they contribute to the people around them.

MCT331 · Design of Mechatronics 1 · Oct–Dec 2025
Cartesian Robotic Sorting & Assembly System

Overview

Fully automated 3-axis Cartesian robotic sorting and assembly system using FreeRTOS on dual ESP32 microcontrollers — integrating pneumatic feeding, dual laser height detection, color sensing, and suction cup end effector. Designed in SolidWorks, simulated in Simscape Multibody with Stateflow, and physically built and demonstrated.

Mechanical Design

  • X-axis: power screw with anti-backlash nut
  • Y-axis: belt-driven DC motor — lightweight high-speed motion
  • Z-axis: lead screw + dual linear rails
  • 20×45mm aluminum extrusion frame, modular trapezoidal lead screws

Control & Electronics

  • Dual ESP32: sensing/classification + motion/actuation
  • FreeRTOS activity-based task sequencing with semaphores
  • ESP-NOW wireless link to remote LCD
  • 5/2 solenoid valve · H-Bridge PWM motor driver · NC fail-safe E-stop

Simulation

  • SolidWorks → Simscape Multibody rigid body import
  • Stateflow cyclic pick-and-place logic
  • Contact force modeling for all product-robot interactions

Tools

SolidWorks · MATLAB Simscape · Stateflow · FreeRTOS · ESP32 · ESP-NOW · C/C++

MCT343 · Intro to Biomechatronics · Dec 2025–Jan 2026
EMG-Based Prosthetic Hand Control

Pipeline

  • JSON → CSV (Python), 4th-order Butterworth bandpass (10–500Hz)
  • Rectification, normalization, DWT (db4, 5 levels)
  • MAV, RMS, WL, iEMG, SSC, Hurst exponent analysis

Channel Selection & Control

  • Index: Ch.8 (RMS=50.6) + Ch.5 · Pinky: Ch.14 (RMS=59.53) + Ch.6
  • Weighted average: 0.55 × dominant + 0.45 × second
  • 3-state Stateflow: Open / Proportional / Strong Grip
  • 5-finger Simscape Multibody with revolute joints

Tools

Python · NumPy · MATLAB · Simulink · Simscape Multibody · Stateflow · Delsys Trigno

Cascaded PID Cart Control

Phase 1 — Single Loop

  • Ziegler-Nichols then manual → PD: Kp=1000, Kd=0.5
  • Motor params via MATLAB Parameter Estimation
  • TF: 1.395×10⁻⁴ / (1.292×10⁻⁸s³ + 0.0576s² + 2.933s)

Phase 2 — Cascaded

  • Outer: displacement · Inner: velocity (RPM)
  • PWM H-bridge + encoder feedback · zero steady-state error
